Privacy policy

Local AI · Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who uses this app

Local AI is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and no other people's accounts are connected to it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's explicit permission through Google's sign-in screen, the app may read Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, Google Tasks, Google Drive files and Google Docs, and may create or change tasks, calendar events, Gmail labels and Drive files that the app itself created. It only requests the permissions the owner chooses to grant.

How the data is used

Data is used only to answer the owner's own requests (for example, "what is on my calendar this week?"). It is processed on the owner's own computer by locally running software and AI models.

Storage and sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Data is not sent to any external AI service.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
